Frequently asked questions

Quick answers about GlowWave FM Chill.

What music does GlowWave FM Chill play?

GlowWave FM Chill plays roots reggae, dancehall, dub and timeless classics, live around the clock.

How do I listen to GlowWave FM live?

Press the play button on this page. No sign-up is needed. You can also use the GlowWave FM app, Apple and Android Auto pages.

Can I listen on my phone or in the car?

Yes. This page works in any modern mobile browser, and GlowWave FM also has dedicated app, Apple and Android Auto pages at app.glowwavefm.com, apple.glowwavefm.com and android-auto.glowwavefm.com.

Does the player have an equalizer?

Yes. The player has a 3-band equalizer with bass, mid and treble sliders from -12 to +12 dB and presets such as Bass boost, Vocal, Night and Bright. Your settings are remembered on this device.

What is the live icon in the browser tab?

While the station plays, the tab icon turns into a small live equalizer that moves with the music, so you can see the stream is running from any tab.

Why does the stream say offline or connection failed?

Check your internet connection and press play again. If it still fails, try the web player at player.glowwavefm.com.
